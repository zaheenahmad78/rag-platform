RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a powerful technique that combines information retrieval with large language models.

Benefits of RAG:
1. More accurate and factual responses
2. Reduced hallucinations
3. Access to up-to-date information
4. Source attribution and transparency

RAG works by first retrieving relevant documents from a knowledge base, then using them as context for the language model.

RAG is widely used in:
- Customer support chatbots
- Research assistants
- Legal document analysis
- Medical information retrieval
