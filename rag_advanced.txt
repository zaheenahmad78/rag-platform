
    RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is an advanced AI technique that combines:
    1. Information retrieval from external knowledge bases
    2. Large language models for response generation
    3. Real-time fact-checking and verification
    
    Key advantages:
    - Reduces hallucinations by grounding responses in retrieved data
    - Enables access to up-to-date information
    - Provides source attribution for transparency
    - Customizable for domain-specific knowledge
    
    Use cases:
    - Enterprise chatbots
    - Research assistants
    - Legal document analysis
    - Medical information systems
